# Learning Center — user discovery notes

Captured: 2026-11-11\
Source: statements from the project team in this Codex conversation\
Use: source material for the draft brief, PRD, and initiative; this is not independent research.

## Statements provided

1. The team wants to build an application called Learning Center.
2. Its purpose is to put employee certifications and training for the United States and Costa Rica in one place.
3. The team wants searches to be easier.
4. Employees are the main audience. The team also wants the product to address HR or learning-administration needs.
5. The product should automatically bring information from other systems into Learning Center.
6. The team also wants people to be able to add information manually.
7. A SharePoint folder named “AI Brownbag - Software” was shared as an example source.
8. The team approved Python as the backend implementation language and React for the frontend; frameworks and versions were not selected.

## Evidence limits

- The current process, its cost, and employee search behavior have not been measured.
- The SharePoint folder's contents, data shape, permissions, and update behavior were not confirmed.
- The team has not identified the full source-system inventory.
- No employee, HR administrator, security, privacy, or IT stakeholder was interviewed in this session.
